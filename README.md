## What Is Kartra? Is It Worth It?

Founded in 2018 and headquartered in San Diego, California, Kartra serves tens of thousands of online businesses worldwide with a platform designed to replace the patchwork of disconnected SaaS tools that most online marketers use.

The platform's founding premise is that the typical online business operator pays for 5–8 separate tools to run their business, a landing page builder, an email marketing platform, a shopping cart, a course platform, an affiliate system, a video host, a helpdesk, and a CRM, and that these tools rarely communicate seamlessly with each other. Kartra replaces all of these with a single integrated platform where every tool shares the same contact database, behavioral tracking, and automation engine.
